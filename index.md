# Fewer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Fewer: Photo Cleaner is published by Dinero Automation LLC. This policy explains what Fewer does with your information. The short version: it stays on your device.

## What Fewer collects
Nothing. Fewer has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting service. It makes no network requests of its own. We cannot see your photos, videos, contacts or calendar, and we receive no data about how you use the app.

## What Fewer reads on your device
With your permission, Fewer reads:
- Photos and videos, to find duplicates, similar shots and other items you may want to remove.
- Contacts, only if you open contact cleanup, to find duplicates.
- Calendar events, only if you open calendar cleanup, to find duplicate and old events.

All analysis happens on your device using Apple's frameworks. Results are stored inside the app on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

## Changes Fewer makes
Fewer deletes, merges or converts items only after you confirm. Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in Photos. Before a contact merge or calendar cleanup, Fewer saves a backup on your device so you can undo it for 30 days.

## Private vault
Items you move to the vault are stored inside the app on your device with iOS file protection. If you delete Fewer, the vault is deleted with it. The vault is included in your device backups like other app data.

## iCloud
If you use iCloud Photos, Apple may download an original to your device when you compress or convert it. That transfer is between your device and Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Fewer does not send anything anywhere.

## Purchases
Fewer Pro is sold by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

## Children
Fewer does not collect information from anyone, including children.
