# Fewer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Fewer: Photo Cleaner is published by Dinero Automation LLC. This policy covers Fewer on iPhone and iPad (App Store) and on Android (Google Play). It explains what Fewer does with your information. The short version: it stays on your device.

## What Fewer collects
Nothing. Fewer has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting service. It makes no network requests of its own, other than asking the App Store or Google Play about your Fewer Pro purchase. We cannot see your photos, videos, contacts or calendar, and we receive no data about how you use the app.

## What Fewer reads on your device
With your permission, Fewer reads:
- Photos and videos, to find duplicates, similar shots and other items you may want to remove.
- Contacts, only if you open contact cleanup, to find duplicates.
- Calendar events, only if you open calendar cleanup, to find duplicate and old events.

All analysis happens on your device, using Apple's frameworks on iOS and Android's own media APIs on Android. Results are stored inside the app on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

## On Android
On Android, Fewer asks for the photos and videos permission (or, if you prefer, access to only the items you select). It does not ask for contacts or calendar, and it has no vault. Fewer's scan results and settings stay in the app's private storage and are not included in Android backups or device transfers. When you delete items, Android shows its own confirmation and moves them to the trash, where they stay for 30 days before Android removes them.

## Changes Fewer makes
Fewer deletes, merges or converts items only after you confirm. Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in Photos on iOS, and to the trash on Android. Before a contact merge or calendar cleanup, Fewer saves a backup on your device so you can undo it for 30 days.

## Private vault
Items you move to the vault are stored inside the app on your device with iOS file protection. If you delete Fewer, the vault is deleted with it. The vault is included in your device backups like other app data.

## iCloud
If you use iCloud Photos, Apple may download an original to your device when you compress or convert it. That transfer is between your device and Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Fewer does not send anything anywhere.

## Purchases
Fewer Pro is sold by Apple through the App Store on iOS, and by Google through Google Play on Android. To check whether you own Fewer Pro, the app asks the App Store or Google Play; that exchange is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your payment details or any information about your library.

## Children
Fewer does not collect information from anyone, including children.
